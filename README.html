🍃 Nirāma (निराम)

AI-Powered FMCG Nutrition & Food Purity Auditor built for Indian consumers. Unmasking disguised sugars, palm oil fractions, and industrial chemical INS additives.

Next.js 15.2 (App Router) React 19 TypeScript 5.6 Google Gemini Multimodal Groq Qwen Engine Cloudflare Pages Edge

🎯 Project Overview & Mission

Over 80% of packaged food products in Indian supermarkets advertise enticing wellness claims such as "Immunity Booster", "High Fibre Digestive", "Real Fruit Juice", or "Diabetic Care". In reality, food formulations are heavily weighted toward ultra-processed ingredients:

  • Disguised Sugars: Common children's nutrition drinks contain 40% to 50% added sugars masked as maltodextrin, liquid glucose, and invert sugar.
  • Refined Palmolein Oil: Commercial biscuits and snacks rely almost universally on high-saturated refined palm oil fractions rather than unrefined oils or traditional ghee.
  • Cryptic INS Codes: Chemical numbers like INS 150c, INS 471, and INS 627 conceal synthetic ammonia-treated caramel colors, gut-barrier disruptors, and excitotoxins.
The Nirāma Solution: A dual-camera label scanner using Google Gemini Flash Vision and Groq Qwen to give shoppers an immediate 1–10 Purity Score, full NOVA processing classification, plain-English additive decoding, and clean Indian whole-food swaps.

✨ Core Features & Scientific Engines

📸 Dual-Camera Multimodal Scan

Accepts front and back packaging images simultaneously, with automated client-side canvas compression ensuring sub-second upload speeds even on 4G networks.

🧬 1–10 Purity Scoring

Calculates a holistic purity rating factoring in NOVA processing degree, additive toxicity profiles, refined sugar density, and cooking fat quality.

🏷️ NOVA International Classification

Maps products against the global NOVA framework: Group 1 (Unprocessed), Group 2 (Culinary Ingredients), Group 3 (Processed), and Group 4 (Ultra-Processed Food / UPF).

🔬 Zero-Hallucination INS Decoder

Deciphers Indian FSSAI and international Codex INS codes (e.g., INS 150c, INS 500ii, INS 471) into their industrial purpose, risk tier, and biological impact on gut health.

🍬 Sugar Radar & Alias Discovery

Converts sugar grams into intuitive household teaspoons and exposes stealth sugar aliases (Maltodextrin, Invert Sugar, Liquid Glucose, Dextrose).

🛢️ Palm Oil & Industrial Fat Alert

Scans the ingredient list for refined palmolein, hydrogenated vegetable fats (vanaspati), and interesterified oils vs wholesome cold-pressed oils and A2 cow ghee.

⚖️ Front-of-Pack Claim Reality Check

Performs a critical truth audit contrasting marketing claims ("Immunity Formula", "Heart Health") directly against back-of-pack formulation realities.

🔄 Clean Indian & Desi Kitchen Swaps

Supplies verified clean packaged alternatives (The Whole Truth, Two Brothers Organic Farms, Slurrp Farm) and authentic whole-food Indian home recipes (Sattu Shake, Makhana Chaat).

🚀 Quick Start & Installation

  1. Clone the repository: 
    git clone https://github.com/your-username/nirama.git
cd nirama
  2. Install project dependencies: 
    npm install
  3. Configure environment keys: Create a .env.local file: 
    GEMINI_API_KEY=your_google_gemini_api_key
GROQ_API_KEY=your_groq_api_key
  4. Launch local development server: 
    npm run dev
    Access the application at http://localhost:3000.

☁️ Cloudflare Pages Deployment

Nirāma is fully configured for zero-cold-start deployment on Cloudflare Pages:

  • Build Command: npx @cloudflare/next-on-pages
  • Output Directory: .vercel/output/static
  • Compatibility Flags: nodejs_compat (specified in wrangler.json)
  • Environment Variables: Configure GEMINI_API_KEY and GROQ_API_KEY in the Cloudflare Pages settings.
